Mechanical Framework

Decoding ANSI/ISEA 138
Top-of-Hand Protection Mandates

The ANSI/ISEA 138 standard establishes precise performance requirements for gloves designed to protect the top of the hand. Unlike older standards that grouped all protection together, this framework specifically isolates the hand's most vulnerable impact zones.

The testing protocol relies on strict mechanical measurements:

Testing Protocols & Core Scales

Zone Drop Tests

Targeted Zone Drop Tests: A controlled mass is dropped onto the knuckles and fingers of the glove with an impact energy of 5 Joules.

Force Transmitted

Transmitted Force Measurement: High-velocity sensors underneath the glove record the exact amount of force (measured in Kilonewtons, or kN) that passes through the protective material to the hand.

Inverse Law

The Inverse Rating Law: In this system, lower transmitted force correlates directly with a higher impact rating. The less energy that reaches the worker's hand, the better the glove performs.

📊 Classification Scale: Gloves are rated from Level 1 (lowest protection, allowing up to 9 kN of force transmission) to Level 3 (highest protection, allowing no more than 4 kN of force transmission).
Protection Tiers

Understanding ANSI/ISEA 138 Impact Levels

The ANSI/ISEA 138-2019 standard classifies back-of-hand impact protection on a strict scale from Level 1 to Level 3. While a Level 1 rating is engineered to mitigate moderate injuries in lighter environments, a Level 3 rating is reserved for high-impact, heavy industrial scenarios.

Traceability Legislation

Strict Marking Requirements for Impact PPE

Permanent traceability is a legal and operational necessity for industrial personal protective equipment. The ANSI/ISEA 138-2019 standard outlines precise on-glove branding rules that differ significantly from other frameworks:

Distinct Functional Scope: While the foundational ANSI/ISEA 105-2024 standard governs multi-hazard properties like mechanical cuts, material abrasions, chemical barriers, and flame resistance, the ANSI/ISEA 138-2019 standard focuses exclusively on back-of-hand impact defense.
Mandatory Performance Display: For a glove to claim official certification under the ANSI 138 framework, it must explicitly display its performance level (Level 1, 2, or 3) directly inside the official icon.
Lifelong Legibility: The standard mandates that this compliance marking must remain completely visible and legible throughout the entire useful lifespan of the glove, ensuring safety inspectors can verify protection tiers even on heavily weathered gear.
