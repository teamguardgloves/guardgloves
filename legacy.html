Our Legacy

BUILT ON HERITAGE. ENGINEERED FOR WHAT’S NEXT. For three generations, our family has evolved from international sports-goods trading into professional glove manufacturing—combining decades of hands-on experience with modern technology, responsible practices, and a commitment to continuous improvement. SINCE 1966

Where

We Started

1966 — WHERE THE JOURNEY BEGAN Our story began in 1966, when our family entered the international sports-goods trade. From our early beginnings, we developed relationships across Africa and the Middle East, gradually expanding into European and American markets. What started from a small office would eventually become the foundation for a much larger manufacturing journey.

  • FROM TRADING TO MAKING As our experience in international markets grew, so did our understanding of what customers needed. The business evolved from trading sports goods to manufacturing, with the second generation taking the important step into professional glove production. We were no longer simply supplying products. We began making them.

THE SECOND GENERATION — FROM EXPERIENCE TO EXPERTISE

  • The second generation established professional glove manufacturing as a core part of our business. Over time, manufacturing capabilities expanded, production processes became more sophisticated, and our knowledge of materials, construction and performance continued to grow. The foundation was set for the next chapter.

THE THIRD GENERATION — BUILDING ON WHAT WE INHERITED

  • Today, the third generation carries that legacy forward. But preserving a legacy does not mean keeping things unchanged. It means taking what previous generations built—and making it better. We introduced modern manufacturing technologies, computerized stitching systems, new product development and stronger quality-management systems.

FROM A SMALL OFFICE TO A MODERN MANUFACTURING NETWORK

  • What began from a small office has evolved into three major manufacturing facilities, each covering more than 200,000 sq. ft. Today, our manufacturing capabilities allow us to take products from raw materials to finished goods under one roof.

THE EVOLUTION OF A GLOVE MANUFACTURER

  • SPORTS GOODS → PROFESSIONAL GLOVE MANUFACTURING → SPECIALIZED PROTECTIVE GLOVES → MODERN OEM MANUFACTURING → INNOVATION & NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT Today, Guard Gloves is focused on manufacturing gloves across a wide range of applications, while continuing to develop products around the specific requirements of our customers and markets.

MADE UNDER ONE ROOF

EXPERIENCE MEETS MODERN MANUFACTURING Manufacturing is at the heart of Guard Gloves. Our facilities combine experienced craftsmanship with modern machinery and production systems designed to improve consistency, efficiency and quality. Computerized Stitching — Modern equipment designed for greater precision and consistency. Lean Manufacturing — Continuous improvement focused on reducing waste and improving efficiency. Modern Machinery — Replacing older equipment with newer technologies as our manufacturing requirements evolve. Integrated Production — Greater control across the journey from raw material to finished product. Product Development — Continually developing and improving products for changing customer requirements.

MORE THAN MANUFACTURING — A RESPONSIBILITY TO DO BETTER

As the business entered its third generation, our definition of progress changed. Growth is not only about producing more. It is about how we produce, how we treat people, how we use resources and how we prepare for the future. Ethical Manufacturing — Building responsible practices into our operations. Social Responsibility — Creating a workplace and supply chain built around responsible standards. Environmental Awareness — Working toward more efficient and environmentally conscious manufacturing. Quality Systems — Developing structured systems that help us deliver consistent products. Continuous Improvement — Constantly looking for better ways to manufacture.

THE NEXT CHAPTER — THE LEGACY CONTINUES

The journey that began in 1966 is still evolving. We continue to invest in manufacturing technology, lean processes, product development, responsible practices and new markets. The next generation of Guard Gloves will be shaped by the same principle that has carried our family business forward for decades: Respect what experience has taught us. Improve what can be better. Build what comes next.
