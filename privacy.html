Privacy Policy

Guard Gloves (“Guard Gloves,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information provided through our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit or interact with the Guard Gloves website.

The website is primarily intended for business-to-business (B2B) customers, including manufacturers, importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, safety companies, sports businesses, brands, and other commercial organizations.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Name and job title
  • Company or organization name
  • Business email address
  • Telephone number
  • Business or shipping address
  • Country and city
  • Product interests, requirements, and structural specifications
  • Information submitted through quotation, sample, OEM/ODM, private-label, or contact forms
  • Communications and correspondence with Guard Gloves
  • Information necessary to process business transactions or provide customer service

We generally collect information directly from you when you submit a form, request a quotation or sample, contact us, or communicate with us regarding our products or services.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to business inquiries
  • Prepare quotations and proposals
  • Provide product information and samples
  • Discuss OEM, ODM, private-label, and custom manufacturing
  • Establish and manage distributor or wholesale relationships
  • Process and fulfill business orders
  • Communicate regarding products, shipments, and services
  • Provide customer support & improve our website and business services
  • Maintain business records
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements
  • Send relevant business or marketing communications where permitted by law
Note: We do not sell personal information for money.

Business Information

Because Guard Gloves operates primarily as a B2B manufacturer, exporter, distributor, and brand owner, we may also receive information about the businesses with which we communicate, such as:

  • Company name & industry details
  • Business location
  • Product requirements & custom specifications
  • Estimated purchasing requirements
  • Distribution interests
  • Shipping and commercial requirements

Business information may be used to evaluate and manage potential or existing commercial relationships.

Sharing Information

We may share personal information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Guard Gloves personnel and relevant business operations
  • Manufacturing and production teams
  • Canadian distribution and customer-service teams
  • Shipping, freight, logistics, and customs providers
  • Website hosting and technical service providers
  • Business communication and email service providers
  • Professional advisers and service providers
  • Government, regulatory, or law-enforcement authorities where legally required

We only share information as reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes, service provision, legal compliance, or protection of our rights.

Cross-Border Operations (Canada & Pakistan)

Guard Gloves operates through business activities in Pakistan and Canada.

  • Pakistan operationsmay include manufacturing, product development, quality control, sourcing, and export activities.
  • Canadian operationsmay include distribution, warehousing, branding, sales, and customer service.

As a result, personal information may be accessed, processed, or transferred between Canada, Pakistan, and other countries where our trusted service providers operate. Where required by applicable law, we take reasonable measures to protect personal information when it is transferred or accessed across borders.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, security, remembering preferences, understanding traffic, and measuring marketing effectiveness.

We may use analytics or advertising services such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, or similar services only where those services are actually implemented on the website.

These services may collect information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location, referral source, and website activity. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Certain website functions may not work properly if essential cookies are disabled.

Marketing Communications

If you provide your business contact information, we may send you relevant business communications regarding products, quotations, new developments, exhibitions, services, or other commercial opportunities where permitted by applicable law. You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions provided in the communication or contacting us directly.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including business, contractual, accounting, legal, regulatory, and dispute-resolution requirements. When information is no longer required, we may securely delete, destroy, or anonymize it where appropriate.

Access and Correction

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information. We may need to verify your identity before processing such a request.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

Children

The Guard Gloves website is designed primarily for business and commercial users and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

Privacy Rights & Complaints

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correction, withdrawal of consent, or other privacy protections. If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please contact us first so that we can investigate and attempt to resolve the matter. Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to remove or restrict privacy rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website, technology, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated “Last Updated” date.
