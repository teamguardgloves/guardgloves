Our Technical Blueprint

At Guard Gloves, circular manufacturing, SASB sustainable metrics, and material science unite to deliver high-performance industrial protection.

Abrasion
Resistant

Guard Gloves safety gloves are engineered with anti-abrasion structural barriers, designed specifically to withstand harsh material handling and high-friction industrial operations.

Tear
Resistant

We engineer our heavy-duty work gloves with high-tensile composite fibers, advanced weaving, high-tensile structural hides, and advanced reinforcement technologies.

Cut
Resistant

By integrating advanced cut-resistant composite liners beneath premium high-tensile hides, Guard Gloves manufactures heavy-duty leather safety gloves that deliver elite blade protection.

Puncture
Resistant

Featuring a puncture-impermeable internal matrix and rugged grain leather, Guard Gloves provides uncompromising mechanical armor against sharp spikes and industrial hazards.

Impact
Resistant

Crafted with targeted impact padding and premium shock-absorbing materials, our gloves provide a powerful layer of defense to shield your hands from heavy jolts and crushing threats.

Fire
Resistant

Crafted with specialized thermal insulation and heat-reflective materials, our gloves provide a powerful barrier to safeguard hands from extreme temperatures and spark hazards.

Cold
Resistant

Engineered for sub-zero environments, Guard Gloves utilizes high-performance, heat-trapping microfibers and a windproof exterior to lock in core warmth during sub-zero operations.

Water
Resistant

Defying wet and muddy job sites, Guard Gloves treats premium leather to shed fluids instantly on contact, keeping hands dry and ensuring a steady, non-slip hold in fluid-heavy conditions.

Engineered for absolute precision and unrivaled hands protection. Delivering world-class handcrafted leather gear globally since 1966.
